One app sits in the middle. People type a question into a web page. The app runs a 7-step pipeline. It talks to three outside services: Claude, the AI that reads and writes; EUR-Lex, the official EU law database; and, only if switched on, Gemini for a second opinion. It keeps two things: a cache of laws it has already read, so each law is read only once, and a usage log of what every answer cost. Every AI call goes through a cost meter, and the app checks the budget before each answer.
