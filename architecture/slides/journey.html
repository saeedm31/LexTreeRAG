From question to answer, in 7 steps

STEP 1 · AI (Haiku)

Sort the question

What kind of question is it? Splits multi-part ones.

STEP 2 · AI (Haiku)

Pick search words

Legal terms, EU synonyms and the year.

STEP 3 · NO AI

Search EUR-Lex

Live search of the official database. Prefers original laws.

STEP 4 · AI (Haiku)

Index new laws

Splits a new law into articles with summaries. Once, then cached.

STEP 5 · AI (Haiku)

Choose articles

Reads only the table of contents. Scores each article 0–10.

STEP 6 · AI (your pick)

Write the answer

Claude Opus by default. Uses only the chosen articles.

STEP 7 · AI (Haiku)

Check the answer

Does it cover the question? Does it stick to the law?

RESULT

The user gets

The answer, article links, a confidence score and its cost.

Low score? Search again, wider (up to 2 times).

Blue boxes use AI (Claude). The grey box uses no AI. The orange box is what the user sees.

This is what happens to one question. Steps 1 and 2: a small, cheap AI model (Claude Haiku) works out what kind of question it is and picks good search words. Step 3: we search the official EU database. No AI here. Step 4: if a law is new to us, Haiku splits it into articles and writes a one-line summary of each. This happens once per law; after that it is cached. Step 5: Haiku reads only that table of contents and scores each article. Step 6: the big model, Claude Opus by default, writes the answer from the chosen articles only. Step 7: Haiku checks the answer. If the score is low, we search again with wider terms, up to two more times, and keep the best answer.
