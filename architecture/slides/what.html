The product

What it does

EU law is huge and written for lawyers. LexTreeRAG finds the article that answers your question and gives you the source.

Finds the right laws

Searches the official EU law database (EUR-Lex) live. No manual browsing.

Reads like a lawyer

Looks at each law’s table of contents and opens only the articles that matter.

Shows its work

Links every answer to the exact articles, scores its own confidence and shows what it cost.

The problem: EU law is spread over thousands of regulations. Finding the one article that answers a question can take hours. LexTreeRAG does three things. It searches the official EU database live. It reads each law the way a lawyer uses a table of contents, opening only the useful articles. And it shows its work: links to the articles, a confidence score, and the cost of the answer.
