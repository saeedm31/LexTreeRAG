A company will ask: what does this cost? So every AI call is counted. The token numbers come straight from the AI provider, so they are exact. Under each answer you see the cost and which step spent it. The Usage tab shows totals over time. A monthly budget gives a warning at 80 percent and stops AI answers at 100 percent. On AWS that budget is per team and checked on the server. Users can also choose a cheaper or stronger answer model and see the price first. Reading a new law costs money once; after that it is reused for free.
